Join the team!

Wanted:
Engineer-in-
Training

Koers & Associated Engineering LTD. is looking to fill civil engineering positions at our company on Vancouver Island. Our roles involve working with team members on a wide variety of municipal infrastructure projects in our local service area. We are looking for motivated and insightful people who enjoy working in a collaborative environment with like-minded people.

Given a steady and ongoing increase in project workload, we are looking to hire an engineer-in-training seeking exposure to interesting and fulfilling civil infrastructure projects.

The Position:

The successful candidate will be directly supervised by a professional engineer (P.Eng.) registered in BC, and generally be responsible for assisting with design, contract administration and project coordination tasks. Responsibilities will generally include the following:

• The preparation of feasibility studies and conceptual designs,
• The design of municipal water, storm and wastewater sewer infrastructure,
• Computer modelling of existing and proposed infrastructure systems,
• Coordinating approval and permit applications,
• Preparation of cost estimates, technical specifications, and construction documents,
• Contract administration and site inspection services.

The Candidate:

The ideal candidate will be self-motivated, demonstrate initiative, and work well in a team environment. The candidate must be registered, or eligible for registration as an EIT with EGBC. Preference will be given to those with the following:

• Degree in Civil Engineering, or similar field,
• 1 to 4 years of progressive project experience,
• Registered, or eligible for registration, with EGBC,
• Experience with software such as Civil 3D, Infowater and XP-SWMM would be an asset,
• A valid BC driver’s licence and access to a vehicle.

What We Offer:

This is a salaried position with an anticipated starting base salary between $65,000 - $85,000 per year, depending on experience and qualifications. We can also offer you:

• Opportunity to gain considerable experience on large, complex, and challenging design and construction assignments,
• Ability to utilize new technology as you advance your career,
• Mentoring and supervision by seasoned and well-respected professionals,
• A friendly and welcoming employee-owned office atmosphere,
• Recognition of effort with regular performance bonuses,
• Attractive remuneration packages, with great benefits, including extended health, dental, short- and long-term disability, and company RRSP contributions.

About Us:

Now in our fortieth year, Koers & Associated Engineering LTD. has been providing consulting engineering services on a variety of civil infrastructure projects on Vancouver Island. We are a local, well-established firm with deep ties to the region and with a strong presence in the municipal infrastructure renewal and development community. Our company is employee-owned, consisting of highly experienced and professional people that have built strong connections with clients and local communities. We encourage ongoing professional development opportunities for all staff and support flexible working at home options.

Location:

Parksville is located 20 minutes north of Nanaimo on the east coast of sunny Vancouver Island where residents enjoy a pleasant climate all year round and convenient access to some of the Island’s most popular destinations. The area is best known for its sandy beaches, world class hiking and biking trails, boating activities, and popular events, such as the Canadian Open Sand Sculpting Competition. The Oceanside area provides a high quality-of-life with many recreational opportunities for all residents.
